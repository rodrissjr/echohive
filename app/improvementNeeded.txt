Honest advice for your demo / report
These are real improvements worth mentioning if asked:

Loading states — right now clicking a category shows nothing while fetching. Add a simple spinner. One useState(false) + wrap refreshFeed with it.

Error handling on actions — handleReact, handleBookmark etc. can silently fail if Supabase rejects. Wrap with try/catch and call showToast(err.message).

Sign out doesn't work — signOut is imported but never called. Find the logout button and wire it to signOut().then(() => setUser(null)).

No pagination — feed loads 30 posts max. Fine for a demo with few users.
